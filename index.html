    06 · About

    Hi, I’m Akash.

    I’m a photographer, videographer and content creator from Chennai, and I shoot where it’s loud: concerts, audio launches, press meets and college fests. From Ilaiyaraaja and Hip Hop Tamizha’s homecoming to Thalapathy Vijay and the Toxic pre-release at Nehru Stadium, I wait for the split second when the light, the crowd and the artist line up.

    It all started on the college stage at Takshashila 2K25. Today I freelance for teams like Behindwoods and The Papster South, and I’m still studying at Chennai Institute of Technology (class of ’28). I’m on the other side of the lens too: I act in reels and I made my debut short film, Ignored.
