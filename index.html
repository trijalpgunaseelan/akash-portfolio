04 · About

Hi, I’m Akash.

I’m a photographer, videographer and content creator from Chennai, and I shoot where it’s loud: concerts, audio launches, press meets and college fests. From Ilaiyaraaja’s “Truly Live” to Thalapathy Vijay at the Sathyabama family wedding, I wait for the split second when the light, the crowd and the artist line up.

Then I turn those frames into content people share. One of my reels crossed 3.2 lakh likes. I’m studying at Chennai Institute of Technology (class of ’28) and take freelance bookings across Tamil Nadu.

“Some events you attend. Some events you experience. And some… you get to capture.”
